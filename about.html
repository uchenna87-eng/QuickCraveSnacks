About Quick Crave Snacks

A family business, built one machine at a time

We started Quick Crave Snacks to bring modern, reliable vending to Chicagoland businesses — with the kind of local, hands-on service the big national operators don't offer.

Our mission

Make snacks and drinks effortlessly available wherever Chicagoland works, heals, and lives — with zero cost or hassle for the businesses that host us.

Our vision

To be the vending partner every Chicago-area office, clinic, school, and apartment building recommends to the next one.

Our values

Show up when we say we will, keep machines clean and stocked, and treat every location like it's the only one we have.

Our story

Quick Crave Snacks started with a simple frustration: too many vending machines in Chicago were poorly stocked, rarely serviced, and impossible to reach when something went wrong.

As a family-owned operation, we do things differently. We answer the phone. We know the routes we run. And because we're local, a jammed machine or an empty tray gets fixed in hours or days — not weeks.

Today we place and service snack, beverage, and combo machines across offices, medical clinics, manufacturing floors, apartment buildings, schools, fitness centers, and hotels throughout the greater Chicago area — always at no cost to the host location, and always with a share of the revenue going back to them.
